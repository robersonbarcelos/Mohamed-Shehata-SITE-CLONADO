For over two decades, I've led design at the intersection of human behavior and business performance — scaling UX teams, shaping product strategy, and making design a boardroom conversation.

At United Electronics Co. (eXtra), I built and led a multidisciplinary UX organization across KSA, Oman, and Bahrain — growing online revenue to SAR 1.22B, launching the Jood loyalty platform, and delivering a zero-regression migration across millions of users.

Outside my corporate role, I founded Enrichly — an AI-powered PDP content platform — and mentor the next generation of UX leaders through ADPList.

I hold the Nielsen Norman Group VP/Director Specialty Track certification, with focus areas in DesignOps, Leading UX Teams, and Lean UX & Agile.
